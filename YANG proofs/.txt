The Week 1 Consultancy Task
The client asks:

How should Northstar modernise the deployment, configuration and operational management of its existing infrastructure while maintaining security, reliability and appropriate administrative control?


From the case study:

"The company wants to reduce its dependence on manual CLI configuration. The consultancy should investigate model-driven network programmability using: YANG → RESTCONF/NETCONF → network device. The PoC should demonstrate retrieving operational/configuration information and making a controlled configuration change. For example: Create or modify an interface/VLAN using RESTCONF and verify the resulting configuration. A small Python application could then retrieve information from several devices."
